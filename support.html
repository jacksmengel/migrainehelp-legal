Frequently Asked Questions

How do I cancel my subscription?
Open iPhone Settings → tap your name → Subscriptions → Migraine Help → Cancel Subscription.

How do I delete my data?
Open Migraine Help → Profile tab → Data & Privacy → Delete All My Data.

Why isn't my risk score updating?
Make sure Apple Health permissions are granted in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → Migraine Help.
