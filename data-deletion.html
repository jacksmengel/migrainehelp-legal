Facebook Data Deletion Instructions

Migraine Help operates as an anonymous utility application. We value your privacy and are committed to maintaining a transparent, secure environment. Because we do not require user account registrations or email sign-ups, we do not collect, store, or possess personal identifying information (such as names, emails, or phone numbers) on our servers.

However, if you interacted with our Meta advertisements and wish to completely remove your anonymous platform activity data from our Meta App integration, you can easily do so through your personal Facebook settings.

According to Meta's platform regulations for apps using the Facebook SDK, you can request the deletion of your activities by following these steps:

  1. Log into your Facebook account and navigate to Settings & Privacy, then select Settings.
  2. On the left-hand side or under your account configurations, scroll down and click on Apps and Websites.
  3. Locate Migraine Help within the list of active or expired applications linked to your profile.
  4. Click the Remove button located next to the app name.
  5. Confirm the removal in the pop-up modal. This action revokes the app's permission to interact with your Meta profile and tells Meta to delete associated interaction logs for this specific application.

If you have any questions regarding your data privacy or encounter issues completing these steps, please reach out to our team at: support@migrainehelpapp.com
